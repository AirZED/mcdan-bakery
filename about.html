Baked with Passion in Uyo

McDan Bakery is a family-friendly bakery on Abak Road, Uyo, dedicated to making fresh, delicious baked goods for every occasion.

From our daily bread and snacks to elaborate custom cakes, everything is made from quality ingredients by people who love what they do.

Our mission: to make every celebration sweeter and every morning better.
